Module 3 · Financial Exposure

Onboarding-Friction Revenue-at-Risk

How each provider fares against industry CAC benchmarks

Since no public install-volume benchmark works across companies this different in size, this compares providers on a normalized basis: estimated annual CAC at risk from onboarding friction, per 10,000 monthly installs, using each provider's own tier benchmark CAC and its own real friction-review prevalence. Traditional banks and neobanks are benchmarked separately because their acquisition economics are structurally different.

Benchmark sources: traditional-bank CAC ≈ £120–£280 (≈$150–$350) per VisBanking and YouYaa; neobank CAC ≈ £4–£25 (≈$5–$30) per the same sources. Review read-through (77% of users read at least one review before installing a free app) and rating-conversion elasticity (a 0.3-star rating drop tracks with a 19% rise in cost-per-install; 50–85% of users won't consider a sub-3-star app) per Business of Apps (Apptentive data) and Screenfast (Coinis, AppTweak data). These are blog-cited industry figures without a single primary source behind them — treat as a plausible range, not an audited statistic, and USD→GBP is a rough ~0.79 conversion.
